# SDD Workflow — Performance Trade-offs and Data Volume

## US-53: Performance Trade-offs Carry Production Data Volume [P0]

As a developer running `prospec-review`,
I want the efficiency lens to ask for production data volume when a change or its plan accepts a cost that grows with data volume,
so that a decision such as "no index" is not carried forward by later reviews before data backs it.

**Acceptance Scenarios:**
- WHEN a diff or its plan accepts a missing index, a full scan or per-row queries and the change artifacts carry no production row counts, growth or total work for it, THEN the reviewer files a `major` finding asking for the data or a way to measure it
- WHEN that decision already cites production data volume, THEN this criterion files no finding
- WHEN the finding stays unfixed, THEN the review loop is not blocked and a human decision recorded in the plan stays in force

---

#### REQ-TEMPLATES-252: The efficiency lens asks for production data volume behind a performance trade-off
The efficiency / performance lens grades a performance trade-off accepted without production data volume behind it. A change or its plan that accepts a cost growing with data volume — a missing index, a full scan, per-row queries — without production row counts, growth or total work at the run frequency as its basis is filed as a `major` finding that asks for that data or a way to measure it, or the ask joins the finding another efficiency criterion files for that cost. The finding concerns the missing basis of an accepted decision: a joined finding carries the higher of the two severities, this criterion never lowers another criterion's severity, and flagging a path as slow without a baseline, when no trade-off was decided, stays `minor`.
- WHEN a diff or its plan accepts such a cost and the change artifacts carry no production data volume for it, THEN the lens files a `major` finding asking for the data or a measurement method, or adds that ask to the finding another efficiency criterion files for the same cost
- WHEN a reviewer only suspects a path is slow and no trade-off decision was taken, THEN the existing slow-path rule applies and this criterion files no finding
- WHEN the decision cites production data volume, THEN this criterion files no finding
- WHEN the data-volume ask stays unanswered, THEN the ask by itself does not block the review loop or overturn a human decision recorded in the plan
- WHEN another efficiency criterion files the same cost at a lower severity, THEN the joined finding is filed at `major`
- WHEN the criterion renders, THEN it names no specific database, ORM or driver

---

#### REQ-TESTS-139: The data-volume criterion is pinned in the efficiency lens section
The data-volume criterion is pinned inside `## Efficiency / Performance Lens` by section-scoped contract assertions on its row, its `major` severity, its recorded-decision clause, its join-the-existing-finding clause with the higher-severity rule, its distinction from the slow-path rule and its vendor-neutral wording.
- WHEN the row is removed or its severity is changed from `major`, THEN a contract test fails, each shown by an applied mutation
- WHEN the recorded-decision clause, the join-the-existing-finding clause, the higher-severity rule or the slow-path distinction is removed, THEN a contract test fails
- WHEN the row names a specific database, ORM or driver, THEN a section-scoped negative assertion fails, shown by an applied mutation
