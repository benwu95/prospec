# SDD Workflow — Installed Review Skill Criteria

## US-54: Review Applies the Criteria of Installed Review Skills [P1]

As a developer whose environment has personal review skills installed,
I want `prospec-review` to pick the matching review skills outside the `prospec-*` set before it spawns the reviewer and hand only their criteria to a generic fresh-context reviewer,
so that my private criteria take part in every round without overriding the Prospec review contract or the project's own conventions.

**Acceptance Scenarios:**
- WHEN the environment has review skills that match this diff, THEN the reviewer receives their criteria within the per-round cap, and a finding those criteria catch names its source in its evidence
- WHEN no installed review skill matches, THEN the round runs on the shipped lenses and a generic delegate with no error and no WARN
- WHEN a personal criterion conflicts with the Constitution, `_conventions.md`, the playbook or established practice in the repository, THEN no finding is filed on it
- WHEN an external skill carries its own report format, severity names or edit steps, THEN the output stays a `ReviewFindingsInputSchema` payload at critical, major or minor and the delegate edits no file

---

#### REQ-TEMPLATES-253: Review adds the criteria of installed review skills under the Prospec contract and project knowledge
`prospec-review` adds the criteria of review skills installed in the developer's environment to each review round, under the Prospec review contract and the project's own knowledge. The rules are review-specific text in the `delegation-protocol` Spawn step — the orchestrator's criteria selection and the brief item the reviewer follows — which the skill's Review Lenses section names as the delegation protocol's criteria selection and review-criteria brief item, reached from The Loop step 1, for delegated and in-session review alike.
- WHEN a review round starts, re-review rounds included, THEN the orchestrator selects, against the cumulative diff, at most three installed review skills outside the `prospec-*` set whose stated scope matches a language, framework or concern the diff touches, skipping a skill that the shipped lens criteria already adapt as their attributed source
- WHEN no installed review skill matches, THEN the round runs on the shipped lenses with no error and no WARN
- WHEN a skill is selected, THEN each of its criteria is assigned to the shipped lens it falls under, and a conditional lens that receives a criterion runs that round and is declared in `--lenses`; the reviewer applies only the criteria and ignores the skill's report format, severity names and edit steps — in reviewer mode B the single reviewer receives the skill's readable file path or its rules quoted verbatim with each rule's assigned lens, in mode A each lens agent receives only the rules assigned to its lens, quoted verbatim
- WHEN a selected criterion yields a finding, THEN it is filed under its assigned lens at `critical`, `major` or `minor` in the `ReviewFindingsInputSchema` payload, its `evidence` starts with `External criterion: <skill> — <rule>`, and any later payload that carries evidence for the finding — a later round's or its critical verifier's — keeps that line first, copied from the finding's `review.md` evidence
- WHEN a selected criterion contradicts the Constitution, `_conventions.md`, the playbook or established practice in the repository, THEN no finding is filed on it
- WHEN the existence of a critical is verified, THEN the verifier is pointed to only the criterion its finding's `review.md` evidence cites
- WHEN the rules render, THEN the spec-architecture lens is still always added by prospec, the Spawn brief's snapshot and main-tree read-only rules are unchanged, and no host tool, agent type or third-party skill is named

#### REQ-TESTS-140: The review-criteria selection and brief item are pinned section-scoped
The review-criteria text is pinned section-scoped: the criteria selection and the review-criteria brief item each appear exactly once in the rendered `delegation-protocol`, inside its Spawn step, together carrying the selection cap, the attributed-source skip, the shipped-lens fallback, criteria-only briefing with its mode A and mode B assignment (each rule's lens included), re-review rounds and conditional-lens activation, both precedence rules, shipped-lens filing, the evidence marker and its rule for every later payload carrying evidence (copied from `review.md`), the verifier rule and the in-session reviewer's coverage, and the `prospec-review` Review Lenses section points to both.
- WHEN any of those clauses is removed from either text, THEN a contract test fails, each shown by an applied mutation
- WHEN either text appears more than once in the rendered reference or outside the Spawn step, THEN a contract test fails
- WHEN the `prospec-review` pointer is removed, THEN a contract test fails
